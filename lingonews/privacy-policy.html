Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2026

LingoNews helps users learn English through news, vocabulary practice, audio, and AI conversation practice. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to users.

Information We Collect

  • Account information, such as email address and authentication provider.
  • Learning profile information, such as selected level, teacher, and learning preferences.
  • Learning activity, such as article progress, vocabulary review items, quiz results, and conversation practice history.
  • Subscription status and entitlement information used to manage Premium access.
  • Voice audio, speech transcript, conversation messages, and selected article or topic context when using conversation practice.

How We Use Information

  • To provide account login, Premium access, and app features.
  • To generate English learning content, speech recognition, text-to-speech, AI conversation responses, and learning feedback.
  • To save learning progress and personalize the learning experience.
  • To maintain app security, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot issues.

Third-Party Services

LingoNews may send conversation practice data to third-party AI and audio providers, including Deepgram, OpenAI, and Google Gemini, for speech recognition, text-to-speech, AI conversation responses, and learning feedback generation. This data is not used by LingoNews for advertising or tracking.

Payments and Subscriptions

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple and/or RevenueCat. LingoNews receives subscription entitlement information to determine Premium access, but does not receive full payment card details.

Data Retention and Deletion

Users may delete their account from within the app. Account deletion removes or disables account-related app data according to the app's deletion flow and legal requirements.
